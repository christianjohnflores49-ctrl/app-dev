## Overview
**Stranger Things** is a science fiction–horror series created by the Duffer Brothers and released on **Netflix**.  
It’s set in the 1980s in the small town of *Hawkins, Indiana*, where strange and supernatural events start to unfold.

---

## Main Characters
- **Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown)** – A girl with telekinetic powers  
- **Mike Wheeler (Finn Wolfhard)** – Eleven’s close friend and leader of the group  
- **Jim Hopper (David Harbour)** – The town’s chief of police  
- **Joyce Byers (Winona Ryder)** – A determined mother searching for her son  
- **Will Byers (Noah Schnapp)** – The boy who disappears in Season 1  

---

## Why I Love It
1. The *80s nostalgia* — from the music to the clothes  
2. The *strong friendships* between the main characters  
3. The mix of **mystery**, **horror**, and **science fiction**  
4. The powerful *character development* of Eleven
